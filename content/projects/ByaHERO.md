Integrated IoT jeepney tracking and passenger counting platform designed to modernize the Philippine commuting experience.

As Lead Developer and Hardware Systems Engineer, I led end-to-end development across software, AI integration, and hardware deployment.

Key features include real-time AI occupancy counting using Raspberry Pi 5, Hailo-8L, and dual 1080p cameras; a native Kotlin Android app with Mapbox for live vehicle tracking and seat availability; and custom ASA 3D-printed enclosures built for UV and heat resistance in public utility vehicle conditions.

Field tests on the Sta. Lucia Grand Mall to SM Taytay route validated GPS reliability and passenger-counting accuracy under real commuting conditions.
